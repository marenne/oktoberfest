Fest Details & FAQ

Are children and families welcome? ▼

Yes! Little ones and family members are warmly welcome to join the celebration.

What if I don't own a Dirndl or Lederhosen? ▼

No problem at all! While Bavarian trachten is enthusiastically encouraged if you want an excuse to dress up, festive casual party attire is completely fine.

What will the beverage selection look like? ▼

We will have authentic German beers on tap and ice, alongside a delicious spread of non-alcoholic craft beers, sparkling ciders, and sodas so expecting mamas and designated drivers can raise a proper glass!

Where should I park? ▼

Street parking is available along Vista Grande Street and neighboring residential roads. Please be courteous to neighbors' driveways! Ridesharing is also great.
